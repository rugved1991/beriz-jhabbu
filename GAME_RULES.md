# Beriz Jhabbu - Game Rules

## Overview

Beriz Jhabbu is a card game for 2-16 players featuring two distinct phases. The game uses multiple standard 52-card decks (one deck per 4 players). The objective is to avoid being the last player with cards.

## Setup

1. Choose number of players (2-16)
2. The game automatically calculates and uses the required number of decks
3. All cards are dealt to players (some players may have one more card than others)
4. The game begins with Phase 1

## Phase 1: Beriz (Addition Phase)

### Objective
Play all cards from your hand while avoiding penalties.

### How to Play

1. Players take turns playing one card to the center table
2. Cards are placed randomly on the table creating a "messy pile"
3. After playing, check if you triggered a penalty (Beriz)

### Penalty Rules (Beriz)

You pick up penalty cards if:

**Duplicate Rank:** Your card's rank (A, 2, 3... K) already exists on the table
- Example: You play a 7, and there's already a 7 on the table
- You collect: Your played card + the matching card(s)

**Sum Match (numbered cards only):** Your card's value equals the sum of other cards on the table
- Ace = 1, numbered cards = face value, face cards (J, Q, K) have no value
- Example: You play a 7, and the table has 2+5 or 3+4 or 1+2+4
- You collect: Your played card + all cards in the matching combination
- If multiple combinations match, you collect the one with the most cards

**BOTH penalties can trigger simultaneously:**
- If your card triggers BOTH a duplicate rank AND a sum match, you collect ALL matching cards
- Example: You play 10, table has another 10, 1, 2, 3, 4
  - Duplicate: The other 10 matches your rank
  - Sum: 1+2+3+4 = 10 matches your value
  - You collect: Your 10 + the other 10 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 6 cards total (not just one penalty or the other)

**Face Cards (J, Q, K):** Only trigger duplicate rank penalties, never sum penalties

### Penalty Cards
- Collected cards go to your "side deck" (hidden from other players)
- Your side deck becomes your hand in Phase 2

### Phase 1 Ends
- When the last player plays their final hand card
- That player collects all remaining table cards
- The table is cleared and Phase 2 begins

## Phase 2: Jhabbu (Shedding Phase)

### Objective
Empty your hand (side deck from Phase 1) first. The last player with cards loses.

### How to Play

1. Your side deck becomes your hand
2. Players play in rounds (tricks)
3. First player plays a card - this suit becomes the "lead suit"
4. All other players must follow the lead suit if they have it
5. If you don't have the lead suit, you are "void" and can play any card

### Normal Round (No Jhabbu Dump)

- All players play one card following suit rules
- **All cards are discarded** (removed from game)
- Everyone successfully sheds their card
- Player with highest card of lead suit leads the next round

### Jhabbu Dump Round

**When you're void in the lead suit**, you can perform a "Jhabbu Dump":

1. **Select all cards of another suit** (minimum 2 cards to trigger Jhabbu)
2. The system automatically **keeps the lowest card** of that suit for you
3. The system gives the **remaining cards as Jhabbu** to the Jhabbu Receiver
4. **"Jhabbu!!" announcement** appears dramatically on screen
5. The player with the highest card of the **lead suit** becomes the "Jhabbu Receiver"
6. The Jhabbu Receiver must collect **all cards** from the round (your Jhabbu cards + everyone else's cards)
7. **The trick ends immediately** when Jhabbu is played (other players don't need to play)
8. You (the Jhabbu Giver) lead the next round with your kept lowest card

**Note:** After giving Jhabbu, you'll see your kept lowest card in your hand. When it's your turn to lead, play that card to start the next round.

**Special case:** If you only have single cards of different suits left, you can give Jhabbu with just one card and get eliminated.

**Edge case:** If you give Jhabbu with your last card (when you only have 1 card and it's not of the lead suit), you are eliminated and the next active player leads.

### Strategic Notes

- Jhabbu Dump is an **offensive move** - you force someone else to collect cards
- Playing a high card is risky if someone might Jhabbu Dump
- Normal rounds help everyone shed cards equally
- Jhabbu Dump rounds create winners and losers

### Multiple Decks Tiebreaker

With multiple decks, two players might play identical cards (same rank and suit from different decks):
- In normal rounds: Both players shed successfully
- In Jhabbu Dump rounds: The **first player** to play that card becomes the Jhabbu Receiver

### Player Elimination

- When your hand is empty, you're eliminated (you've won!)
- Eliminated players are skipped in turn order
- Game continues with remaining players

## Winning and Losing

- **Winners:** All players who empty their hands
- **Loser:** The last player still holding cards
- The game ends when only one player has cards remaining

## Card Rankings

**Highest to Lowest:**
- Ace (highest in Phase 2 tricks, value of 1 in Phase 1 sums)
- King, Queen, Jack (no numeric value in Phase 1)
- 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2

## Quick Reference

### Phase 1 (Beriz)
- ✅ Play cards from hand
- ❌ Avoid duplicate ranks
- ❌ Avoid sum matches
- 🎯 Goal: Empty your hand (but you'll collect penalties)

### Phase 2 (Jhabbu)
- ✅ Follow suit if possible
- ✅ Jhabbu Dump when void (offensive move)
- ❌ Avoid being Jhabbu Receiver
- 🎯 Goal: Empty your hand first

## Glossary

- **Beriz:** Penalty in Phase 1
- **Jhabbu:** Phase 2 of the game
- **Jhabbu Dump:** Dumping multiple cards when void in lead suit
- **Jhabbu Giver:** Player who dumps cards (offensive)
- **Jhabbu Receiver:** Player who must collect dumped cards (defensive)
- **Lead Suit:** Suit of the first card played in a round
- **Void:** Having no cards of a particular suit
- **Side Deck:** Hidden penalty cards from Phase 1

---

**Tip:** Phase 1 is about managing penalties. Phase 2 is about strategic card play and timing your Jhabbu Dumps!
